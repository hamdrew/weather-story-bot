## Description (BotFather /setdescription, max 512 characters)

I watch the National Weather Service for new Weather Stories, the forecast graphics your local NWS office publishes ahead of notable weather, and post each one to a Telegram channel, usually within 15 minutes.

I only post to channels and don't reply to messages, so there's nothing to do here. Join the channel to get the stories.

Unofficial. Not affiliated with the National Weather Service or NOAA. Images and text come from weather.gov.
