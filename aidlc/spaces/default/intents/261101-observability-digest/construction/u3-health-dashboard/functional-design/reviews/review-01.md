The composite rules match FR1.1 exactly (missed-runs in ALARM or any of the four in INSUFFICIENT_DATA gives UNKNOWN; errors, quiet or repost-loop in ALARM gives NOT HEALTHY). The unit stays inside `infra/`, leaves `monitoring.tf` alone, and the layout arithmetic adds up (11 + 4 + 3 + 3 = 21, charts end at 33, every row sums to 24 columns, 13 widgets everywhere). Two things should be settled before approval: the "0 rather than blank" tile promise has no mechanism, and the alarm-annotation-only chart is asserted as fact when it is not verified. The rest are small.
