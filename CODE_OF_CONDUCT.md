# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Respectful, constructive, empathetic collaboration is expected. Harassment,
discrimination, trolling, personal attacks, publishing private information, or
other unprofessional conduct is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to [@hamdrew](https://github.com/hamdrew), without
sensitive operational or credential material. The maintainer may moderate,
remove, reject, or ban contributions. Adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
